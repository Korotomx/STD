What does the quadratic form xᵀAx look like geometrically?  
How do eigenvalues determine the shape of a surface?  
  
In this video, we present a geometric interpretation of quadratic forms.  
By visualizing xᵀAx as a surface in three-dimensional space, we see how different real symmetric matrices give rise to paraboloids, valleys, ridges, and saddle surfaces.  
  
Although these surfaces appear very different, they all originate from the same matrix expression xᵀAx.  
The algebraic properties of the matrix—positive definite, negative definite, semi-definite, or indefinite—are revealed directly through geometry.  
In this way, abstract matrix theory becomes something you can literally see and reason about.  
  
By examining surface shapes and contour plots, we gain intuitive insight into how the eigenvalue structure of a matrix controls growth and decay along different directions.  
This provides a unified geometric picture connecting quadratic forms, eigenvalues, and surface geometry.

二次型 xᵀAx 在几何上究竟意味着什么？  
矩阵的特征值如何决定曲面的形状？  
  
在这个视频中，我们从几何视角理解二次型。  
当 x 是二维向量、A 是实对称矩阵时，二次型 xᵀAx 可以看作一个二元函数，  
并在三维空间中表现为不同形状的曲面。  
  
向上或向下开口的抛物面、山谷面、山脊面以及典型的马鞍面，  
看似完全不同，但它们都来源于同一个矩阵表达式 xᵀAx，  
区别只在于矩阵 A 的代数性质不同。  
  
通过观察曲面的形状以及对应的等高线，我们可以直观理解：  
正定、负定、半正定、半负定以及不定矩阵，在几何上的本质区别。  
矩阵的特征值结构，决定了曲面在不同方向上的“上升”或“下降”行为，  
从而将原本抽象的代数定义，转化为可以看见、可以想象的几何直觉。
