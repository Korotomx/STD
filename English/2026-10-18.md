## gradient
- /ˈɡreɪdɪənt/ · n. 梯度；坡度
- **the gradient of a function** 函数的梯度：The gradient points in the direction of steepest ascent.
- **compute the gradient** 计算梯度：Backpropagation computes the gradient of the loss.

## criteria
- /kraɪˈtɪrɪə/ · n. 标准，条件（criterion 的复数）
- **meet / satisfy the criteria** 满足标准：The candidate meets all the selection criteria.

## criterion
- /kraɪˈtɪriən/ · n. 标准，准则
- **the main criterion for sth.** 某事的主要标准：Speed is the main criterion for a cache.

## optimizer
- /ˈɒptɪmaɪzə/ · n. 优化器（机器学习中更新模型参数的算法）
- **an optimizer such as Adam** 像 Adam 这样的优化器：Adam is a widely used optimizer for training networks.

## epoch
- /ˈepək/ · n. 时期，时代；（机器学习）完整遍历一遍训练集
- **train for N epochs** 训练 N 个 epoch：The model was trained for 50 epochs.

## batch
- /bætʃ/ · n. 一批；（机器学习）一小批样本 / v. 分批处理
- **a batch of jobs / requests** 一批任务 / 请求：The worker processes jobs in batches.
- **batch size** 批大小：Lower the batch size if you run out of memory.
