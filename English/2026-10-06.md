## irreverent
- /ɪˈrevərənt/ · adj. 不敬的，无礼的
- **an irreverent tone / joke** 不敬的语气 / 玩笑：The blog takes an irreverent tone toward tech trends.
- **irreverent toward authority** 对权威不敬：He's irreverent toward authority but brilliant at his job.

## best practice
- 最佳实践，最佳做法
- **follow / adopt best practice** 遵循最佳实践：The docs list best practices for writing safe code.
- **industry best practice** 行业最佳实践：Using version control is an industry best practice.

## generalized lvalue
- 广义左值：C++ 里扩展的左值概念，指能出现在赋值号左边的更广义的表达式
- **a generalized lvalue** 广义左值：In C++, a generalized lvalue can appear on the left side of an assignment.

## rvalue
- n. 右值（计算机里不能取地址、通常位于赋值号右边的值）
- **an rvalue reference** 右值引用：Move semantics rely on rvalue references.
- **prvalue / xvalue** 纯右值 / 将亡值：An rvalue can be a prvalue or an xvalue.
