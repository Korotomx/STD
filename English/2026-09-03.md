## thematic
- /θɪˈmætɪk/ · adj. 主题的，主题性的
- **a thematic thread** 贯穿始终的主题：Memory management is a thematic thread that runs through the whole course.

## knack
- /næk/ · n. 诀窍；本领
- **have a knack for sth.** 有做某事的天赋：She has a knack for explaining complex algorithms in plain words.

## cue
- /kjuː/ · n. 提示，暗示；线索
- **take one's cue from sb.** 照某人的样子做：The compiler takes its cue from the type declarations.

## queue
- /kjuː/ · n. 队列
- **a queue of tasks** 任务队列：The scheduler keeps pending jobs in a queue.

## enqueue
- /ɪnˈkjuː/ · v. 入队
- **enqueue a task** 把任务加入队列：The producer enqueues a new task for each request.

## dequeue
- v. 出队
- **dequeue an element** 从队列取出元素：The worker dequeues the next job and runs it.

## undershoot
- /ˌʌndərˈʃuːt/ · v. 未达到（目标）
- **undershoot the target** 低于目标：The estimate undershoots the true running time.

## as an aside
- 顺便说一句，题外话
- **As an aside, …** 顺便一提：As an aside, this trick also works in Python.

## bottommost
- /ˈbɑːtəmˌmoʊst/ · adj. 最底部的
- **the bottommost layer** 最底层：Errors often hide in the bottommost layer of the stack.

## override
- /ˌoʊvərˈraɪd/ · v. 覆盖；重写；优先于
- **override a method** 覆盖方法：The subclass overrides the base method to change its behavior.

## tray
- /treɪ/ · n. 托盘
- **the system tray** 系统托盘：The app minimizes to the system tray instead of closing.

## dictionary
- /ˈdɪkʃəneri/ · n. 字典；词典
- **a hash dictionary** 哈希字典：A dictionary maps keys to values in constant time.

## go-to
- /ˈɡoʊ tuː/ · adj. 首选的，必用的
- **one's go-to tool** 某人首选的工具：Stack Overflow is my go-to resource for debugging.

## functional equivalence
- n. 功能对等
- **achieve functional equivalence** 实现功能对等：The two implementations achieve functional equivalence despite different code.

## requisite
- /ˈrekwɪzɪt/ · adj. 必备的，必需的
- **a requisite for sth.** 某事的必要条件：Linear algebra is a requisite for machine learning.

## unilateral
- /ˌjuːnɪˈlætrəl/ · adj. 单方面的
- **a unilateral decision** 单方面决定：The team made a unilateral decision to drop the feature.

## bloat
- /bloʊt/ · v. 膨胀；使臃肿
- **bloat the binary** 使程序变大：Unused libraries bloat the final binary.

## squash
- /skwɑːʃ/ · v. 压扁；消除（bug）
- **squash a bug** 修复漏洞：We squashed three bugs before the release.

## allege
- /əˈledʒ/ · vt. 宣称，断言
- **be alleged to do sth.** 被指控做某事：The company is alleged to have copied the code.

## tar
- /tɑːr/ · n. 焦油；柏油
- **a tar archive** tar 压缩包：Use tar to bundle the source files into one archive.

## depth-first search
- 深度优先搜索：一种图遍历算法，沿一条路径走到尽头再回溯。
- **run a depth-first search** 进行深度优先搜索：Depth-first search explores a path to its end before backtracking.
