## consistent
- /kənˈsɪstənt/ · adj. 一致的，始终如一的
- **be consistent with** 与……一致：The results are consistent with our earlier findings.
- **consistent naming / style** 一致的命名 / 风格：Use consistent naming throughout the codebase.

## empirical
- /ɪmˈpɪrɪkl/ · adj. 凭经验的，实证的
- **empirical evidence / results** 实证证据 / 结果：The claim is backed by solid empirical evidence.
- **an empirical study** 实证研究：They ran an empirical study of sorting algorithms.

## paraphrase
- /ˈpærəfreɪz/ · n./v. 释义，改述
- **paraphrase a question / passage** 改写问题 / 段落：Paraphrase the question before you answer it.
- **a close paraphrase** 高度相似的改写：A close paraphrase still counts as plagiarism.

## plagiarism
- /ˈpleɪdʒərɪzəm/ · n. 剽窃，抄袭
- **commit plagiarism** 抄袭：Students who commit plagiarism fail the course.
- **plagiarism detection** 抄袭检测：The school runs plagiarism detection on every essay.

## synthesis
- /ˈsɪnθəsɪs/ · n. 综合；合成
- **a synthesis of A and B** A 与 B 的综合：The paper is a synthesis of theory and practice.
- **speech synthesis** 语音合成：Modern speech synthesis sounds almost human.

## validity
- /vəˈlɪdəti/ · n. 有效性，正确性
- **the validity of an argument / result** 论点 / 结果的有效性：We questioned the validity of the survey results.
- **a validity check** 有效性校验：The form runs a validity check on each input field.

## vis a vis
- 相对于，关于；与……相比
- **vis-à-vis sth.** 关于某事 / 与某事相比：The article compares Rust vis-à-vis C++ for systems work.
