## srand
- n. 随机数生成器的初始化函数（C 语言中为伪随机数生成器设置种子）
- **call srand(time(NULL))** 用当前时间做种子初始化随机数：Call srand once before generating random numbers.

## leftover
- /ˈleftoʊvər/ · n. 剩余物
- **leftover from sth.** 某事的遗留物：These variables are leftovers from an earlier version.

## indentation
- /ˌɪndenˈteɪʃn/ · n. 缩进；压痕
- **consistent indentation** 一致的缩进：Python uses indentation to mark code blocks.
