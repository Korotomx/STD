## post-training quantization
- /poʊst ˈtreɪnɪŋ ˌkwɑːntəˈzeɪʃn/ · n. 训练后量化（PTQ） · [vocabulary.com ↗](https://www.vocabulary.com/dictionary/post-training%20quantization)
- **post-training quantization (PTQ)** 训练后量化：Post-training quantization reduces a trained model to 8-bit weights without retraining.
- **quantize after training** 训练后量化：PTQ is attractive when the full training pipeline is unavailable.

## quantization-aware training
- /ˌkwɑːntəˈzeɪʃn əˈwer ˈtreɪnɪŋ/ · n. 量化感知训练（QAT） · [vocabulary.com ↗](https://www.vocabulary.com/dictionary/quantization-aware%20training)
- **quantization-aware training (QAT)** 量化感知训练：QAT simulates low-precision arithmetic during training so the model adapts to quantization error.
- **QAT vs. PTQ** 量化感知训练与训练后量化：QAT usually preserves more accuracy than PTQ at the same bit width.

> 词库原条目拼作 “qutization”，正确拼写为 quantization。
