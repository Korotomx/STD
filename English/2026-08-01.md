## dissipate
- /ˈdɪsɪpeɪt/ · v. 使消散，驱散；（精力、金钱）挥霍
- **dissipate heat/energy** 散发热量 / 能量：The heatsink dissipates heat away from the CPU.
- **dissipate one's energy** 浪费精力：Don't dissipate your energy on trivial arguments.

## agree upon
- 就……达成一致
- **agree upon a plan / terms / price** 就计划 / 条款 / 价格达成一致：The two sides finally agreed upon a price.

## transistor
- /trænˈzɪstər/ · n. 晶体管
- **a transistor radio** 晶体管收音机：Transistors replaced bulky vacuum tubes in the 1950s.

## so to speak
- 可以说，打个比方说
- **…, so to speak** 可以说：He's the backbone of the team, so to speak.

## acronym
- /ˈækrənɪm/ · n. 首字母缩略词
- **an acronym for sth.** ……的首字母缩略词：“RAM” is an acronym for Random Access Memory.
