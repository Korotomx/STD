## quantization-aware training
- /ˌkwɑːntɪˈzeɪʃən əˈwer ˈtreɪnɪŋ/ · n. 量化感知训练（词本原条目拼作 qutization，正确拼写为 quantization） · [vocabulary.com ↗](https://www.vocabulary.com/dictionary/quantization-aware%20training)

- **QAT vs PTQ** 量化感知训练与训练后量化：QAT usually recovers more accuracy than PTQ but costs another training run.
