## paradigm shift
- 范式转移，思维模式的根本转变
- **a paradigm shift in sth.** 某领域的范式转移：Transformers caused a paradigm shift in NLP.

## gadget
- /ˈɡædʒɪt/ · n. 小装置，小玩意
- **a handy gadget** 方便的小工具：This little gadget measures the Wi-Fi signal strength.

## disruptive innovation
- 破坏性创新（以更简单、更低价的方案颠覆现有市场的创新）
- **a disruptive innovation** 颠覆式创新：The smartphone was a disruptive innovation for cameras.

## radical innovation
- 根本性创新（基于全新原理或技术的重大创新）
- **a radical innovation** 根本性创新：The transistor was a radical innovation, not a small tweak.
