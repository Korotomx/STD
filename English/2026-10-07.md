## hurdle
- /ˈhɜːrdl/ · n. 障碍，难关；（跨栏赛的）栏架
- **clear / overcome a hurdle** 克服障碍：The team cleared the final hurdle before launch.
- **a major hurdle** 一大障碍：Funding remains a major hurdle for the startup.
