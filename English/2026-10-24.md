## TFLOPS
- /ˈtiːflɒps/ · n. 太浮点运算每秒（Tera Floating Point Operations Per Second，衡量算力的单位） · [vocabulary.com ↗](https://www.vocabulary.com/dictionary/TFLOPS)
- **peak TFLOPS** 峰值浮点算力：The card advertises 80 TFLOPS of FP16 throughput, but real kernels rarely reach half of that.
- **count in TFLOPS** 以 TFLOPS 计：We compare GPUs by TFLOPS, yet memory bandwidth usually decides the actual speed.


## Instructions per second
- /ɪnˈstrʌkʃnz pər ˈsekənd/ · n. 每秒指令数（IPS，衡量 CPU 速度的单位） · [vocabulary.com ↗](https://www.vocabulary.com/dictionary/instructions%20per%20second)
- **millions of instructions per second** 每秒百万条指令：MIPS was a popular way to quote CPU speed in the 1980s.
- **instructions per second tells only half the story** 每秒指令数只说了一半：Two CPUs with the same instructions per second can differ hugely once cache misses are counted.

## parallelization
- /ˌpærəlelɪˈzeʃən/ · n. 并行化，并行处理（把任务或操作改为并行执行） · [vocabulary.com ↗](https://www.vocabulary.com/dictionary/parallelization)

## Bernstein condition
- /ˈbɜːrnstiːn kənˈdɪʃn/ · n. 伯恩斯坦条件（两条语句可并行执行的充分条件：无共享变量的读写冲突） · [vocabulary.com ↗](https://www.vocabulary.com/dictionary/Bernstein%20condition)
- **satisfy the Bernstein condition** 满足伯恩斯坦条件：Two statements can run in parallel only if they satisfy the Bernstein condition — no read-write conflict on shared variables.
- **check Bernstein's conditions** 检查伯恩斯坦条件：Before parallelizing a loop, check Bernstein's conditions statement by statement.


## domain decomposition
- /doʊˈmeɪn ˌdiːkɑːmpəˈzɪʃn/ · n. 域分解，区域分解（把计算区域切成子域分给各处理器） · [vocabulary.com ↗](https://www.vocabulary.com/dictionary/domain%20decomposition)
- **domain decomposition across blocks** 按线程块做域分解：Each thread block owns a tile of the image, so domain decomposition maps naturally onto the grid.
- **a domain decomposition scheme** 域分解方案：The stencil solver splits the grid into slabs — a classic domain decomposition.


## functional decomposition
