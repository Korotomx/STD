## saturation
- /ˌsætʃəˈreɪʃn/ · n. 饱和度
- **color saturation** 色彩饱和度：Increasing saturation makes the colors pop.

## hue
- /hjuː/ · n. 色调
- **adjust the hue** 调整色调：The hue slider shifts the entire color wheel.

## image filter
- 图像滤波器：对图像每个像素做运算以得到新图像的处理过程。
- **apply an image filter** 应用图像滤波器：A blur image filter smooths out high-frequency noise.

## greyscale
- /ˈɡreɪskeɪl/ · n. 灰度
- **convert to greyscale** 转为灰度：Converting to greyscale keeps only the luminance.

## Sobel operator
- 索伯算子：图像处理中用于边缘检测的一种算子。
- **apply the Sobel operator** 应用索伯算子：The Sobel operator highlights edges by estimating gradients.
