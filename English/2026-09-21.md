## rote learning/memorization
- 死记硬背
- **rote memorization** 死记硬背：Understanding beats rote memorization when debugging.

## nuanced understanding
- 细致入微的理解
- **a nuanced understanding of sth.** 对某事的细致理解：A nuanced understanding of pointers separates juniors from seniors.

## setup
- /ˈsetʌp/ · n. 设置；配置
- **the dev setup** 开发环境配置：Her dev setup includes a linter and a formatter.

## here is a slight asterisk here
- 这里有个小星号（表示有个例外或保留意见要说明）
- **there's an asterisk** 有个例外要说明：The comparison holds, but there's an asterisk for Unicode strings.

## undersell
- /ˌʌndərˈsel/ · vt. 低估；低价抛售
- **undersell oneself** 贬低自己：Don't undersell your work in the interview.

## residue
- /ˈrezɪduː/ · n. 残留；剩余
- **a residue of sth.** 某事的残余：The refactor left a residue of dead code.

## residue of experience
- 经验的沉淀（用户随手记的碎片）
- **the residue of experience** 经验的沉淀：Intuition is the residue of experience in code review.

## make headway into
- 在……上取得进展
- **make headway into a problem** 在问题上取得进展：We finally made headway into the segmentation fault.

## aliase
- n. 别名（alias 的拼写变体）
- **create an alias** 创建别名：The command creates an alias for the long path.

## foothold
- /ˈfʊthoʊld/ · n. 立足点，据点
- **gain a foothold** 站稳脚跟：The language gained a foothold in data science.

## encompass
- /ɪnˈkʌmpəs/ · vt. 包含，涵盖
- **encompass a range** 涵盖一系列：The course encompasses everything from syntax to systems design.

## brief pitch
- 简短的推介（用户随手记的碎片）
- **give a brief pitch** 做简短推介：Give a brief pitch for your idea in one sentence.

## plow forward
- 埋头前进，硬着头皮继续
- **plow forward** 继续推进：When the tests fail, we plow forward and fix them one by one.
