## denial of service
- 拒绝服务（用海量请求使服务器瘫痪的攻击方式）
- **a denial-of-service attack** 拒绝服务攻击：The site went down after a denial-of-service attack.

## terrifically useful
- 极其有用
- **terrifically useful** 极其有用：This library is terrifically useful for parsing logs.

## factual
- /ˈfæktʃuəl/ · adj. 事实的，基于事实的
- **factual errors / accuracy** 事实错误 / 事实准确度：Check the model's output for factual errors.

## crunch
- /krʌntʃ/ · n. 短缺，紧张局面；嘎吱声 / v. 嘎吱作响；密集处理数据
- **a data crunch** 数据处理（密集计算）：The server is doing a big data crunch right now.
- **in a time crunch** 时间紧张：We're in a time crunch to ship before the deadline.

## bulge
- /bʌldʒ/ · n. 鼓起，凸出部分 / v. 鼓起，凸出
- **bulge with sth.** 塞得鼓起来：His pockets bulged with cables and adapters.

## bulging
- /ˈbʌldʒɪŋ/ · adj. 鼓起的，隆起的
- **a bulging folder / inbox** 塞满的文件夹 / 收件箱：Her bulging inbox took a whole weekend to clear.
