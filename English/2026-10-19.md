## touch on
- 提及，谈及
- **touch on a topic / point** 谈到某个话题：The lecture touched on regularization only briefly.

## touch back on
- 回头再谈，重新回到（某个话题）
- **touch back on sth.** 回头再谈某事：Later we'll touch back on the bias-variance tradeoff.

## desensitized
- /ˌdiːˈsensətaɪzd/ · adj. 麻木的，不敏感的（desensitize 的过去分词）
- **be desensitized to sth.** 对某事麻木：Users have become desensitized to endless pop-ups.

## permeate
- /ˈpɜːrmieɪt/ · v. 渗透，弥漫
- **permeate every layer of** 渗透到……的每一层：The idea permeates every layer of the design.

## self-explanatory
- /ˌself ɪkˈsplænətɔːri/ · adj. 不言自明的
- **be self-explanatory** 不言自明：The function name is self-explanatory.

## perceptron
- n. 感知机（最简单的单层神经网络模型）
- **a single-layer perceptron** 单层感知机：A single-layer perceptron can only separate linearly separable data.

## forward propagation
- 前向传播（神经网络中数据从输入层向输出层逐层计算的过程）
- **a forward propagation pass** 前向传播过程：During forward propagation, each layer passes its output to the next.

## linearity
- /ˌlɪniˈærəti/ · n. 线性，线性度
- **the linearity of a system / model** 系统 / 模型的线性：Adding nonlinearities breaks the linearity of the model.

## nonlinearities
- n. 非线性（nonlinearity 的复数）
- **introduce nonlinearities** 引入非线性：Activation functions introduce nonlinearities into the network.

## deviation
- /ˌdiːviˈeɪʃn/ · n. 偏差，偏离
- **standard deviation** 标准差：The standard deviation measures how spread out the data is.
- **a slight deviation from** 与……略有偏差：Any deviation from the spec must be documented.

## quantifying
- /ˈkwɒntɪfaɪɪŋ/ · n./v. 量化（quantify 的 -ing 形式）
- **quantifying uncertainty** 量化不确定性：Quantifying uncertainty is key to reliable predictions.

## quantifying loss
- 量化损失
- **quantifying loss** 量化损失：Quantifying loss with a cost function drives the training loop.

## gradient descent
- /ˈɡreɪdɪənt dɪˈsent/ · 梯度下降（沿负梯度方向迭代求函数最小值的优化算法）
- **run / apply gradient descent** 运行梯度下降：Gradient descent updates the weights to reduce the loss.

## convergence
- /kənˈvɜːrdʒəns/ · n. 收敛，会聚
- **check convergence** 检查收敛：Plot the loss curve to check convergence.

## gloss over
- v. 掩饰，一笔带过
- **gloss over a problem / detail** 对问题轻描淡写：The report glosses over the model's failure cases.

## with respect to
- /wɪð rɪˈspekt tu/ · 关于；相对于
- **with respect to sth.** 关于 / 相对于某事：Take the derivative of the loss with respect to each weight.

## topology optimization
- 拓扑优化（在给定约束下优化材料分布或结构布局的方法）
- **topology optimization** 拓扑优化：Topology optimization finds the lightest shape that still holds the load.

## convex
- /ˈkɑːnveks/ · adj. 凸的，凸面的
- **a convex function / problem** 凸函数 / 凸优化问题：A convex problem has a single global minimum.

## non-convex
- 非凸的
- **a non-convex loss landscape** 非凸损失曲面：Deep networks optimize over a non-convex loss landscape.

## take it with a grain of salt
- 持保留态度，不完全相信
- **take sth. with a grain of salt** 对某事持保留态度：Take the vendor's benchmark numbers with a grain of salt.

## stochastic
- /stəˈkæstɪk/ · adj. 随机的
- **stochastic gradient descent** 随机梯度下降：Stochastic gradient descent samples a random batch each step.

## middle ground
- 折中，中间立场
- **find a middle ground** 找到折中方案：We found a middle ground between speed and accuracy.

## overfitting
- n. 过拟合（模型过度记住训练数据、泛化能力差）
- **prevent / avoid overfitting** 防止过拟合：Dropout helps prevent overfitting on small datasets.

## intricacies
- /ˈɪntrɪkəsiz/ · n. 错综复杂的细节
- **the intricacies of sth.** 某事的复杂细节：The talk digs into the intricacies of attention.

## regime
- /reɪˈʒiːm/ · n. 体制，制度；状态，情形
- **a low-data regime** 低数据的情形：The method works well in a low-data regime.

## plateau
- /plæˈtoʊ/ · n. 高原；稳定水平 / v. 达到平台期
- **reach a plateau** 到达平台期：The model's accuracy reached a plateau after 20 epochs.

## hierarchical
- /ˌhaɪəˈrɑːrkɪkl/ · adj. 分层的，等级体系的
- **a hierarchical structure / model** 层级结构 / 模型：ConvNets learn hierarchical features from edges to objects.

## sequential data
- 顺序数据（按时间或位置排列、顺序重要的数据）
- **model sequential data** 对顺序数据建模：RNNs are designed to model sequential data.

## modality
- /moʊˈdæləti/ · n. 形式，形态；（多模态中的）一种信息类型
- **a modality such as text or image** 文本或图像这样的模态：A multimodal model fuses text and image modalities.

## time-resolved
- adj. 时间分辨的
- **time-resolved measurement / data** 时间分辨测量 / 数据：Time-resolved data shows how the signal evolves.

## building from the ground up
- 从零开始搭建
- **build sth. from the ground up** 从零搭建某物：They built the compiler from the ground up.

## feed-forward
- adj. 前馈的（数据单向流动、无循环的网络结构）
- **a feed-forward network** 前馈网络：A feed-forward network passes data straight through without loops.

## concretize
- /ˈkɑːnkriˌtaɪz/ · v. 使具体化
- **concretize an idea** 把想法具体化：A proof of concept concretizes the design.

## RNN
- 循环神经网络（Recurrent Neural Network）
- **train an RNN** 训练 RNN：An RNN processes a sequence one token at a time.

## pertinent
- /ˈpɜːrtnənt/ · adj. 相关的，切题的
- **pertinent to sth.** 与某事相关：Include only details pertinent to the bug report.

## tokenization
- 分词，令牌化（把文本切成 token 的过程）
- **the tokenization step** 分词步骤：Tokenization splits the text into words or subwords.

## mitigate
- /ˈmɪtɪɡeɪt/ · v. 减轻，缓解
- **mitigate a risk / problem** 降低风险 / 缓解问题：Caching mitigates the latency problem.

## similarity metric
- 相似性度量（衡量两个对象相似程度的指标）
- **a similarity metric such as cosine similarity** 相似性度量：Cosine similarity is a common similarity metric for embeddings.
